About

A CA who understands both the law and the business.

CA Abhishek Bajaj is a practising Chartered Accountant based in Hyderabad, registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI). He is the founder of Abhishek Bajaj & Co., a full-service CA firm, and also runs Smartbooks Global — a US-focused accounting and bookkeeping practice serving clients across India and internationally.

What sets Abhishek apart is his ability to bridge traditional Indian compliance practice with modern, technology-enabled delivery. He brings hands-on expertise across a wide range of engagements — from statutory audits and GST appeals to FEMA filings, ROC compliance, and cross-border advisory for businesses with international operations.

He holds a QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor certification (Level 1 & 2) and an Intuit Bookkeeping certification, and has worked with clients across India, the United States, Singapore, Dubai, and Switzerland. His approach is rooted in professional rigour, clear communication, and genuine long-term commitment to every client he serves.
