COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

How do I know which service I need?

Use the service finder or share the issue you are trying to resolve. We can discuss relevant support before defining the engagement.

Can we work together remotely?

Yes. The information exchange, meeting format and secure document-sharing process are agreed for each engagement.

Are fees displayed online?

Fees are agreed after reviewing scope, complexity, volumes and timelines. A written proposal is provided before work begins.

Does a consultation request confirm a booking?

No. It expresses your preference. Appointments are confirmed separately after availability and scope are reviewed.

Where should I send sensitive documents?

Do not send passwords, identity documents or bank records through a general enquiry. Ask for the secure sharing channel for your engagement.

Do you handle international requirements?

We support accounting, financial advisory and Indian cross-border compliance. Local statutory responsibilities and specialist coordination are agreed in scope.
